The reason this matters to a manufacturer is narrow and practical. A distributor without a finance function cannot extend credit to a surgeon. A distributor without an import operation cannot hold demonstration stock. A distributor without a regulatory unit waits on a third party for every change to a registration. ACT does not outsource any of the three.

02 · Coverage

Five cities, in person

Colombian plastic surgery is not concentrated in one city, and a surgeon in Barranquilla does not buy a system from someone who visits twice a year.

Bogotá
Head office, training base and the largest concentration of the specialty
Medellín
Second market for body contouring and a strong private clinic sector
Cali
Established aesthetic surgery centre with significant international patient flow
Barranquilla
Coastal hub, and the access point for the north of the country
Bucaramanga
Growing specialty base and a teaching presence

Each team makes face to face contact with the surgeons in its region. That is what turns a demonstration into a sale, and it is the difference between a brand that is available in Colombia and a brand that is present in it.

03 · Finance

Credit, and why it is a commercial weapon

The barrier in this market is not desire for the equipment. It is the cost of capital in front of the surgeon.

Rates in Colombia have moved against the buyer, and surgeons have become reluctant to commit cash to a purchase of this size. A distributor who can only offer the manufacturer's terms is limited to the surgeons who can pay immediately, and that is a small and shrinking group.

ACT runs its own credit assessment and portfolio management. That makes structured financing and direct credit to the surgeon a real offer rather than an intention, and it widens the addressable market to practices that would otherwise never reach a decision.

This is also what makes the payment terms requested in the proposal work. Terms from MicroAire to ACT are what allow terms from ACT to the surgeon. The chain only holds if both ends of it exist.

04 · Regulatory

An internal regulatory unit

Registration work is where distribution quietly stalls, and it stalls fastest when it is handled by somebody else's licence.

ACT has its own regulatory and quality unit dealing with INVIMA directly: obtaining registrations, adding references to an existing one, and handling transfers of title. It also holds the practical knowledge of how the process is actually moved through, which is not the same thing as knowing the rules.

ACT commits to keeping everything regulatory relating to MicroAire compliant with local rules. That is offered as a capability ACT brings, not as a claim on anything that belongs to MicroAire.

05 · Logistics

Imports, warehousing and demonstration stock

Nationalisation, bonded handling, warehousing and inventory control, run in house.

This covers complete systems, handpieces and the consumable lines, and it covers the demonstration kits that make a sale possible in the first place. A surgeon decides on a system after using it, which means somebody has to own, insure, move and maintain the equipment he uses to decide. That sits with ACT.

06 · Service

A local authorised centre

The single largest source of friction in this market today is what happens after something breaks.

ACT is preparing a local authorised maintenance centre in alliance with Novatécnica, with operations projected to begin in Q1 2027. The point is to replace a reactive model, where a failure becomes a six week export and an unpredictable bill, with a standardised one: preventive maintenance under factory protocols and a repair turnaround measured in days.

This protects MicroAire's brand equity more than it protects ACT's margin. A surgeon whose equipment is out of service for six weeks does not blame the distributor. He blames the brand, out loud, to the colleagues who were considering it.

07 · Education

How the technique spreads

Power assisted lipoplasty is not sold by specification. It is adopted by surgeons who have seen it used by a surgeon they respect.

  • Workshops and hands on training. Intensive courses and live demonstrations in theatre with opinion leaders, so that first contact with the system is a surgeon using it rather than reading about it.
  • Webinars and a digital library. Virtual sessions on high definition sculpting technique and on the use of the specialised cannula family, available after the fact rather than only live.
  • University and faculty access. Sessions at schools with plastic surgery programmes, aimed at residents. It sells nothing this year, and it is how the base looks in a decade.
08 · Marketing

Brand presence, and the part that is not digital

Marketing in this specialty is a stand, a demonstration and a conversation as much as it is a campaign.

  • Congresses. A branded, high impact stand at the national congress of the Sociedad Colombiana de Cirugía Plástica and at the body contouring symposia, plus SAPS and TDABS, the two largest international meetings held in Colombia. ACT designs, builds, brands and staffs it.
  • Sponsorship and speakers. Active sponsorship of the meetings that matter, and respected surgeons presenting the technology to their peers.
  • Sustained digital. Continuous campaigns on Instagram and direct email to the surgeon base, run as a programme rather than as occasional posts.
  • The relationships underneath all of it. The reason a surgeon walks over to the stand is that he already knows the person standing at it.
09 · The point

Why this page exists

The proposal asks MicroAire to move the representation in Colombia to ACT. That is a reasonable thing to hesitate over, and the honest answer to the hesitation is not a promise. It is a structure that already exists.

Sales teams in five cities. A finance function that can extend credit. An import and warehousing operation. A regulatory unit that deals with INVIMA directly. A service answer with a partner and a date. An education programme with faculty access. And the surgeon relationships, which are the one item on this list that could not have been bought or hired.
